Police Radio Scanner + – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Police Radio Scanner + ("the app", App Store ID 966533522) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your settings are stored on your device. We do not sell your data.

2. Location (optional)

If you allow location access ("While Using the App"), the app uses your approximate location (about 1 km accuracy) to show scanner stations near you. To find your region, the location is converted to a place name using Apple's geocoding service on iOS. We do not receive or store your location. If no nearby stations are found, the name of the region (for example the state) may be recorded in anonymous app analytics (see section 6). You can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Scanner feeds (Broadcastify)

Station listings and live audio come from Broadcastify. Requests for station data are sent through our server function hosted on Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, EU region), which forwards them to Broadcastify. Live audio is streamed from Broadcastify's servers, which, like any web server, receive your IP address. Station data is also loaded from a public file hosted on Google. Audio can keep playing in the background.

4. App integrity (Firebase App Check)

To protect our server function from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check with Apple's App Attest, which confirms to the server that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

5. Advertising for free users (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device and app information, IP address (which Google may use to estimate your approximate location) and how you interact with ads. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

6. App analytics and configuration (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example which screens and features are used) so we can improve it. We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings without an app update. This data is used in aggregate and is not linked to your identity.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase is active.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
