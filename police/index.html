Police Radio Scanner + – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to all current versions of the app; the consent message is available from version 2.5.7

This policy explains how the iOS app Police Radio Scanner + ("the app", App Store ID 966533522) handles personal data.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by the team behind Police Radio Scanner+ ("we", "us").
Contact for all privacy questions and requests: appsinsights@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number in the app.
  • Scanner audio and station lists come from Broadcastify and other radio servers. Requests for Broadcastify data go through our server function in the EU.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads and app analytics only use your device storage after you choose in Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your settings, saved stations and premium status are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location (optional). If you allow location access ("While Using the App"), the app uses your location on the device to find nearby scanner stations. It converts the location to a place name using Apple's geocoding service. We do not receive or store your location. If no nearby stations are found, only the name of the region (for example the US state) may be sent with an app-analytics event (section 7). You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Scanner feeds, radio streams and station data

  • Broadcastify scanner data. To list scanner feeds and live calls, the app sends the feed or group identifier and a position or time range to our server function on Google Cloud (Firebase Cloud Functions, region europe-west1, Belgium). The function forwards the request to Broadcastify (api.bcfy.io). No identifier of you or your device is included. Google, as our hosting provider, processes your IP address and technical request data to run the function.
  • Live audio is streamed directly from Broadcastify's servers and, for some music and radio stations in the catalogue, from those stations' own streaming servers. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and technical data (such as device type and app version in the request). They are independent providers.
  • Station list and logos. The app downloads a public station list file hosted on Google (Google Sheets), and station logos from our web host (digidot.co). These servers receive your IP address.

5. App protection (Firebase App Check)

To protect our server function from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check with Apple's App Attest. App Attest confirms that a request comes from a genuine copy of the app. Google processes an attestation token from Apple and technical request data for this.

6. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Starting with version 2.5.7, when you first open the app in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent tool using the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework).

  • You can consent, not consent, or manage options per purpose and per ad partner.
  • Ads start only after this step.
  • App analytics (section 7) runs only if you give consent for measurement.
  • Your choice is stored on your device.
  • You can change or withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. It does not affect processing that happened before.
  • If you do not consent, the app still works and shows limited ads. Limited ads don't use personal data to personalise ads; Google still uses your IP address to deliver the ad, and may use it to prevent fraud.

7. App analytics, configuration and experiments (Google Firebase)

Analytics. With your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland), or by default elsewhere, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It collects:

  • a random app instance ID, created on install and reset when you reinstall;
  • usage events, such as listening started, a stream error, the paywall shown or closed, a plan selected, and a purchase started, completed, cancelled or failed, together with the product, price and currency;
  • whether you have premium;
  • the paywall version you saw;
  • device model, iOS and app version, language;
  • an approximate location (country or city) that Google derives from your IP address.

We do not set a user ID and do not combine this data with your name or contact details.

Purchase events. When you buy or renew a subscription, the app reports the transaction (product, price, currency, date) to Firebase Analytics as a purchase event. Apple processes the payment itself. We never receive your name or payment card details.

Remote Config and A/B tests. We use Firebase Remote Config to change app settings without an update. We use Firebase A/B Testing to compare versions of the in-app purchase screen (for example, layout and annual price).

  • Remote Config uses a random Firebase installation ID and your device's country, language and app version to decide which settings or test group apply. It also helps keep you in the same group.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app loads these settings only after the consent step. If you have not consented to analytics, you get the standard version and are not part of the test.
  • We only use test results in aggregated form.

8. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process:

  • device and app information;
  • the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and other app-bound identifiers;
  • your IP address (from which Google may estimate an approximate location);
  • your interaction with ads;
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

Whether ads are personalised, non-personalised or limited depends on your consent choices (section 6) and on Google's rules. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Premium subscribers see no ads, and for them the ad SDK is not started.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · the list of ad partners is shown in the consent message.

9. In-app purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's terms. The app checks with Apple (StoreKit) whether a purchase is active, and stores the result on your device. Purchase events are also sent to Firebase Analytics (section 7).

10. Feedback and contact

If you choose to send feedback, the app opens a draft in your mail app with the app version, iOS version and current feed name filled in. You decide whether to send it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to answer you.

12. Who receives data

  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd.; Google LLC, USA).

- Processor on our behalf for Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config, A/B Testing, App Check, Cloud Functions, Installations), under Google's data processing terms.
- Independent controller for AdMob advertising and the consent message, under Google's controller terms.
- Analytics data may also be used by Google for its own purposes only to the extent our Google Analytics data-sharing settings allow.

  • Ad partners listed in the consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Each is an independent controller for what it receives through Google's ad auction.
  • Broadcastify and the radio stations' streaming servers, independent providers, as described in section 4.
  • Apple, for App Store purchases, App Attest and geocoding, under Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with data brokers.

13. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Google LLC and some ad partners and stream providers are located in the USA or other countries outside the EEA. Transfers to Google LLC rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified. Where it doesn't apply, they rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Google's terms. Our server function runs in the EU (europe-west1), but Google may access data from outside the EEA under these safeguards.

14. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the app. Your consent choice is kept until you change it, until Google's consent message asks again (for example, after the ad partner list changes), or until you delete the app.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event-level data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Server function logs (Google Cloud): about 30 days.
  • AdMob and ad partners: according to their own policies.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to handle your request, and at most 12 months after the conversation ends.

15. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data, have it corrected or erased, or restrict its processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy options).

Because the app has no accounts, we can usually identify analytics data only through the app instance ID on your device. Write to appsinsights@gmail.com and we will help, including with requests to Google. Deleting the app removes all app data from your device, and reinstalling creates new random IDs.

You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or to the data protection authority where you live or work.

16. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country in the EEA (13–16).
