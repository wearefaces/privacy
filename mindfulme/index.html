Mindful Me: Daily Affirmations – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Mindful Me: Daily Affirmations ("the app", App Store ID 6758354591) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account, your data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your app data and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. The app shows no ads, and we do not sell your data.

2. Reminders and push notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We also use OneSignal (OneSignal, Inc.) to send optional push notifications. OneSignal receives your device's push token, a OneSignal subscription ID and basic device information (such as device model, iOS and app version, language and time zone). Notifications are only shown if you allow them in the iOS prompt. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

More: OneSignal Privacy Policy.

3. App analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (such as device model, iOS and app version and technical details of the crash) so we can fix problems. This data is not linked to your identity.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Photos and sharing

If you choose to save or share an affirmation image, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library or to share it (for example to Instagram Stories or via the iOS share sheet). Images are created on your device and are not sent to us.

5. Fonts

The app may download fonts from Google Fonts, which, like any web server, receives your IP address.

6. In-app purchases and ratings

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details.

The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
