Retro LCD: Wolf & Eggs – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Retro LCD: Wolf & Eggs ("the app", App Store ID 6787676054) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account, game data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your scores and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We do not sell your data.

2. Advertising for free users (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device and app information, IP address (which Google may use to estimate your approximate location) and how you interact with ads. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you buy Remove Ads, no ads are shown.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. App analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (for example app opens). This data is not linked to your identity.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details.

5. Ratings and sharing

The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt, and you can share the app using the iOS share sheet. These are handled by iOS.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

7. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
