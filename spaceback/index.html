SpaceBack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Your photos stay on your device

SpaceBack asks for access to your photo library so it can find exact duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos for you to review. All analysis happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to our servers, or shared with anyone.

Nothing is deleted without you

SpaceBack never deletes anything automatically. Items are removed only when you select them and confirm, and iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted as usual.

What we collect

Nothing. SpaceBack has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any data off your device.

What is stored on your device

The app saves only your appearance choices (accent colour and app icon) in its own local settings. It reads your device's free storage space only to show it to you.

Children

SpaceBack does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
