SpaceBack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Your photos stay on your device

SpaceBack asks for access to your photo library so it can find exact duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos for you to review. All analysis happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to our servers, or shared with anyone.

Nothing is deleted without you

SpaceBack never deletes anything automatically. Items are removed only when you select them and confirm, and iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted as usual.

Contacts (optional)

If you open Contacts cleanup, SpaceBack asks for access to your contacts to find duplicates by name, phone number and email. Contacts are processed only on your device and are never uploaded or shared. SpaceBack does not read contact notes.

Contacts are merged only after you review and confirm the result. Before every merge, the app saves a backup of the original cards on your device (protected with iOS data protection) so you can undo the merge. Backups are deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier if you delete them in Settings. After an undo, restored cards may get new internal identifiers, so they may need to be re-added to Favorites. Cards from different accounts (for example iCloud and Exchange) are not merged.

Subscriptions

SpaceBack Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; it renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

What we collect

Nothing. SpaceBack has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any data off your device.

What is stored on your device

The app saves only your appearance choices (accent colour and app icon) in its own local settings, a local scan cache (so scans can resume) and, if you use Contacts cleanup, the merge backups described above. All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. It reads your device's free storage space only to show it to you.

Children

SpaceBack does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
