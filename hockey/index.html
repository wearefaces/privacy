Hockey Shots Counter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Hockey Shots Counter ("the app", App Store ID 6739985455) handles information. The app is published by We Are Interfaces AB ("we", "us"), and we are responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account, data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your shot counts, goals, history, settings and similar app data are stored only on your device (iOS local storage). To show your progress in the home screen widget, the app shares this data with its widget through an iOS App Group, which also stays on your device. We have no servers that receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising for free users (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process: device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendor), IP address (which Google may use to estimate your coarse, approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads (advertising data). We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Before any ads are requested, we show Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept, reject or customise your choices. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. If you decline personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Premium removes all ads

If you buy Premium, the app shows no ads and the advertising SDK is not loaded or started at all, so no data is sent to Google for advertising.

4. App analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand, anonymously, how the app is used (for example which screens and features are used) so we can improve it. Firebase Analytics only runs after you have given consent; if you do not consent, it stays switched off. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). The data is used in aggregate and is not linked to your identity. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. No tracking

The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

6. Push notifications (OneSignal)

We use OneSignal (OneSignal, Inc.) to send optional push notifications, such as training reminders. OneSignal is only started after you have given consent and allowed notifications in the iOS permission prompt. It then processes your device's push token and a OneSignal device ID, along with basic device information, so notifications can be delivered. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

More: OneSignal Privacy Policy.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Premium) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only stores on your device whether Premium is active, and can restore purchases through Apple.

8. Summary of data types

Depending on your choices, the following data may be processed by the services above. None of it is linked to your identity, and none of it is used for tracking:

  • Device identifiers (identifier for vendor, Firebase installation ID, push token / OneSignal ID)
  • Product interaction (anonymous app usage, via Firebase Analytics with consent)
  • Advertising data (ads shown and ad interactions, via Google AdMob, free version only)
  • Coarse location (approximate location estimated from IP address by the ad provider, free version only)

9. Legal bases (GDPR)

Analytics, push notifications and personalised advertising: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)). Purchases: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), handled by Apple.

10. Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Data processed by Google and OneSignal is kept according to their own retention policies (linked above); Firebase Analytics data is kept for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts and we hold no data that identifies you, the simplest way to remove all app data is to delete the app from your device. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy options and turn off notifications in iOS Settings. For any request, contact us and we will help, including with requests to Google or OneSignal. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or your local data protection authority.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: We Are Interfaces AB, appsinsights@gmail.com.
