Imposter Game - Find the Liar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Imposter Game - Find the Liar ("the app", App Store ID 6755700602) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Games played on one device, the player names you enter and your settings are stored on your device. We do not sell your data.

2. Online games (Firebase Realtime Database)

If you host or join an online game, the game is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database so that all players can take part. This includes the game code, the player names you enter, a random player ID, the game settings and the game state (such as category, secret word, roles, votes and timestamps). This data is used only to run the game.

3. Advertising for free users (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device and app information, IP address (which Google may use to estimate your approximate location) and how you interact with ads. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. App analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used so we can improve it, for example games started and completed, online games created or joined, categories and screens used, language changes, shares, ads shown and purchase events. This data is used in aggregate and is not linked to your identity.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Apple Search Ads attribution

On first launch, the app uses Apple's AdServices framework to ask Apple whether it was installed from an Apple Search Ads campaign. If so, the campaign, ad group, keyword and country returned by Apple are recorded in app analytics. This does not use the advertising identifier.

6. Push notifications (OneSignal)

We use OneSignal (OneSignal, Inc.) to send optional push notifications. OneSignal receives your device's push token, a OneSignal subscription ID and basic device information (such as device model, iOS and app version, language and time zone), plus the date the app was last opened. Notifications are only shown if you allow them in the iOS prompt. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

More: OneSignal Privacy Policy.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
