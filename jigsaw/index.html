Jigsaw Solitaire - Puzzle Game – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Jigsaw Solitaire - Puzzle Game ("the app", App Store ID 6755365236) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account, nothing collected by us

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. We, the developer, do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or other personal details, and we do not run servers that receive personal data from the app. Your settings and game progress are stored on your device; deleting the app deletes them.

The app is free and shows ads. The advertising partner (Google) collects some data through its software in the app, as described in section 2. This is why the app's App Store privacy label lists data as collected.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve, limit, measure and secure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers: an app- or developer-scoped identifier, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (section 3). Where allowed, this may be used to show personalised ads and to link your activity across other companies' apps and websites (on the App Store label: "Data Used to Track You").
  • Coarse location: your approximate location (for example country or city), derived from your IP address. The app does not ask for or use precise GPS location.
  • Ad and app interaction data: which ads were shown, taps on ads and how you interact with the app around ads.
  • Diagnostics: crash data, performance data and other technical data about the ad software.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. We only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob: ad technology partners.

3. Your choices: consent and tracking

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's advertising services process your data according to Google's EU User Consent Policy, and the app may ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use the data in section 2 for serving, frequency limits, measurement and fraud prevention, but not to build an advertising profile. When it is shown, Google's consent message itself processes approximate location, performance data and interaction data to work. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by contacting us at appsinsights@gmail.com, and you can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and reset your advertising identifier.

Tracking (App Tracking Transparency). On iOS, the app can only access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking when Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown. If you decline, or no prompt is shown, the IDFA is not available to the app or to Google. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Remove Ads. The optional Remove Ads purchase turns off ads in the game. The advertising software may remain part of the app and may still process the diagnostic data described above.

4. In-app purchases

The optional purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase is active and can restore it through Apple.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

Ads: storing or accessing information on your device and personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (section 3). Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and diagnostics rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Purchases: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), handled by Apple.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
