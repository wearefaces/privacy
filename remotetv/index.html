Remote TV – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Remote TV - WiFi Control ("the app") handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. We do not sell your data.

2. Local network access to find your TV

The app uses iOS local network access to discover and control TVs on your Wi-Fi network. This discovery and control traffic stays on your local network: information about your TVs and network never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

3. Widget (App Group)

The app shares data with its home screen widget through an iOS App Group. This shared data stays on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device and app information, IP address and how you interact with ads.

Advertising identifier (IDFA). The advertising identifier is only accessed if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not used.

Consent (EEA). In the European Economic Area, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown so you can accept, reject or customise your choices for ads.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used so we can improve it, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (such as device model, iOS and app version, and technical details of the crash) so we can fix problems.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Contact: appsinsights@gmail.com.
