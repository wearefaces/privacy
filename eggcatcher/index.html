Egg Catcher: Wolf Arcade – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Egg Catcher: Wolf Arcade ("the app", App Store ID 6776805671) handles information. The app's developer ("we", "us") is responsible for the information described here (the data controller).

1. No account, game data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your scores and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We do not sell your data.

2. Advertising for free users (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device and app information, IP address (which Google may use to estimate your approximate location) and how you interact with ads. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, so iOS does not give it the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you buy Remove Ads, no ads are shown.

We do not use your data to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and we do not share it with data brokers. The app does not ask for tracking permission, so iOS gives neither the app nor Google your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Depending on your choices in Google's consent message and on the law where you live, Google may show personalised or non-personalised ads; see Google's policies linked below.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent (Google consent message)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, which uses the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) the first time you open it. Until you make a choice, the app does not request ads and does not send analytics, crash reports or Remote Config requests. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited, and analytics, crash reporting and Remote Config are switched on or stay off. If the app cannot reach Google to show the message and you have not made a choice before, it does not request ads and keeps these features off, and tries again later; if you have, your earlier choice is used.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time. Tap the ВРЕМЯ / TIME button on the device to open Settings, then choose “Privacy options” (shown in regions where a consent choice is required, such as the EEA and UK). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, when you withdraw consent, analytics and crash reporting stop and unsent crash reports are deleted. In other regions where "Privacy options" is shown, analytics and crash reporting may continue to run within the choices you make there. "Privacy options" is shown where Google requires it.

To show the message and remember your choice, Google's consent tool uses your IP address to work out your region and stores your choice (the IAB TC string) on your device.

In regions where no consent message is required, ads, analytics, crash reporting and Remote Config are active from the first launch, and the app tells Google Analytics not to use the data for ad personalisation. To stop all collection, delete the app; to ask for deletion of data already collected, contact us (see "Your rights").

4. App analytics and configuration (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played so we can improve it. The app sends events such as game results (score and game mode), offers shown and tapped, ads shown, ad impressions (ad_impression: ad platform, ad format, ad unit, the estimated ad revenue, its currency and precision), and purchase, failed-purchase and restore events. Except for ad impression events, which carry only the ad data listed above, they are sent together with the app version, your device's language and region setting, whether the app is ad-free, a random session ID created each time the app starts, a session counter and gameplay and ad counters (for example games played and ads shown per session, total ads and offers shown, run duration, and the ad network), and, for purchases, the product only (no price or transaction data). Firebase attaches its own app-instance identifier and derives an approximate location from your IP address. This data is associated with that identifier on your device, but not with your name, email or any account (the app has none).

We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust offers and settings without an app update. To do this, the app sends a Firebase installation ID (Firebase Installations), the app version, your device's OS version, language, country and time zone. Firebase Sessions (part of Firebase Crashlytics) records when an app session starts and when the app goes to the background, with app and OS information, SDK version, network connection type and the Firebase installation ID, so that crashes and stability can be measured per session.

Retention: analytics event data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer. Firebase installation IDs are kept until they are deleted. More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Analytics, Remote Config and Sessions only run after you have given consent where consent is required (see §3). Otherwise the app uses built-in default settings.

After you withdraw, until the app is restarted the SDK may contact Google to check its settings; no analytics events are sent.

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report containing the stack trace, the state of the app at the time of the crash, the device model, OS version, free memory and disk space, app version, a Crashlytics installation identifier, and a short log of recent analytics events from that session. Crash reports are used only to keep the app stable. They are not linked to your identity, and we do not add any user ID to them. Crash reports are only sent after you have given consent where consent is required. Before you make a choice, crash reporting is not started, so no crash report is recorded; if you decline, it never starts, and if you withdraw consent, it is switched off and unsent reports are deleted. If a Remote Config rollout is active, the report also says which rollout variant the app received. Firebase keeps crash reports and the identifiers attached to them for 90 days. More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details. The app stores on your device whether Remove Ads is active and can restore purchases through Apple. When a purchase or restore completes, the app records an analytics event with the product only, no price or transaction data (see §4); Apple's transaction details and your Apple ID stay with Apple.

7. Summary of data collected

  • Linked to the app-instance / installation identifier on your device: approximate location (from IP address), device identifiers (Firebase app-instance and installation IDs; identifiers used by Google's ad SDK), purchase history (Remove Ads purchase events), product interaction (game, offer and ad events), advertising data (ads shown and ad impressions), and other usage data (ad-free status, session information). Used for third-party advertising by Google (location, identifiers, product interaction, advertising data), analytics (all of these) and app functionality such as the consent message, Remote Config and crash fixing (location, identifiers, product interaction, other usage data). Purchase history is used for analytics only.
  • Not linked to you: crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data (Crashlytics, Firebase Sessions, Google's ad SDK). Used for analytics, app functionality and ad delivery.
  • Tracking: none. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, payment details or any content you create.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

Advertising, analytics, crash reporting and Remote Config in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: your consent, given in the consent message and withdrawable at any time in Settings → Privacy options (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules). Showing limited or non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)). Purchases: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), processed by Apple.

9. Ratings and sharing

The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt, and you can share the app using the iOS share sheet. These are handled by iOS.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To withdraw consent, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options (see §3). Because the app has no accounts, the simplest way to remove app data from your device is to delete the app. For any request, contact us and we will help. In the EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

11. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes and contact
