FAQ

  1. My TV isn't found / won't connect: Ensure your phone and TV are on the same Wi‑Fi network (not a guest network, and VPN is off). Make sure the TV is powered on and that network control/remote control apps are allowed in the TV's settings (on some TVs this may be called “Control by mobile app” or similar). On your iPhone, allow Local Network access for the app in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. If needed, restart the app and your Wi‑Fi router.
  2. Restore purchases: Open the app's premium/paywall screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy.
  3. Cancel a subscription: On iPhone go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Universal TV Remote > Cancel Subscription (or from the app's Settings choose “Manage subscription”). Cancelling at least 24 hours before renewal avoids the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel.
